KaKontrata: Estimator PH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

KaKontrata: Estimator PH ("the app") is a construction materials calculator and quote maker for Filipino contractors, published by MAK-CT CONSTRUCTION OPC ("we"). Summary: the app works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. No data collection

We do not collect names, contact details, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or any other personal information. The app contains no ads and no analytics.

2. No account, no registration

The app has no login or registration. We have no user database.

3. On-device storage only

Material prices you edit and quotes you build are saved in your device's local app storage so they are there the next time you open the app. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

4. No internet required

The app functions fully offline and makes no network requests.

5. Sharing is user-initiated

When you tap "I-share ang Quote," your device's own share sheet sends the quote text to the app you choose (for example, Messenger). We are not part of that transmission and never see its contents.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience utility tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Future versions

If a future update adds features that change any of the above (for example, optional ads), we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before that version is released.

8. Disclaimer on estimates

Outputs are material quantity estimates only, based on standard Philippine estimating references. They are not structural designs or engineering advice. Consult a licensed civil engineer for design and a local supplier for current prices.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the date above. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's update notes.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: makctapp@mak-ct.com
