KaKontrata: Estimator PH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

KaKontrata: Estimator PH ("the app") is a construction materials calculator and quote maker for Filipino contractors, published by MAK-CT CONSTRUCTION OPC ("we"). Summary: all your estimating data stays on your device — we never see your quotes or prices. The free app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects limited technical data (advertising ID, IP address, ad-interaction events) to serve and measure those ads. That is the only data that leaves your device, and it goes to Google, not to us.

1. No data collected by us

We do not collect names, contact details, location, usage analytics, or any other personal information. We have no servers and no user database.

1a. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of KaKontrata shows a banner ad and, at most once per session, a full-screen ad after you add a result to your quote, served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and app-interaction data (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped), and shares it with Google and its advertising partners. KaKontrata does not request location permission, so the ads SDK cannot access your location. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Where the law requires it, a consent prompt is shown before any ad is loaded. Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2. No account, no registration

The app has no login or registration. We have no user database.

3. On-device storage only

Material prices you edit and quotes you build are saved in your device's local app storage so they are there the next time you open the app. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

4. Works offline

Every calculator and the quote builder function fully offline. The only network requests the app makes are the ad requests described in section 1a; when there is no signal, the app simply shows no ad.

5. Sharing is user-initiated

When you tap "I-share ang Quote," your device's own share sheet sends the quote text to the app you choose (for example, Messenger). We are not part of that transmission and never see its contents.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience utility tool for adult builders and contractors and is not directed at children.

7. Version history

2026-11-09: added the Advertising section — Google AdMob introduced in version 1.2. Earlier versions showed no ads.

8. Disclaimer on estimates

Outputs are material quantity estimates only, based on standard Philippine estimating references. They are not structural designs or engineering advice. Consult a licensed civil engineer for design and a local supplier for current prices.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the date above. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's update notes.
